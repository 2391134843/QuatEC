   weights and learnable rotations. This standalone package reorganizes the
   implementation, provides S/D/M presets, uses JSON identifier mappings and
   sparse labels, and selects checkpoints using a validation holdout from the
   training split. These modifications should not be attributed to the
   PoinnCARE authors. No endorsement by the upstream authors is implied.
   Material adapted from PoinnCARE retains its attribution and noncommercial
   conditions.
